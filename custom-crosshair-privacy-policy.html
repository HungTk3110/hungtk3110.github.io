1. Overview & Commitment

Custom Crosshair (Package: com.obito.armpro) is a specialized gaming utility app designed to assist players by generating an adjustable, custom-designed aiming reticle (crosshair HUD overlay) on screen during gameplay for first-person shooter (FPS) and third-person shooter (TPS) games.

We respect your privacy and are committed to full transparency regarding our data practices. We operate in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, specifically including the User Data Policy, Device and Network Abuse Policy, and Permissions Policy.

2. Personal Information Collection

Explicit Declaration: The developer does NOT directly collect, sell, lease, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from users of Custom Crosshair.

We do not require account registration, login credentials, or social media logins. Specifically, we never collect:

  • Personal identifiers (names, postal addresses, phone numbers, email addresses).
  • Real-time geographic location (Precise GPS or approximate cell tower/Wi-Fi location).
  • Biometric, financial, microphone, camera, or contact list data.
  • Personal photos, media files, or internal device storage documents.

4. Android Permissions & Justifications

In compliance with the Google Play Permissions Policy, the following section discloses every sensitive and runtime Android permission requested by Custom Crosshair, detailing the strict, core user-facing functionality each permission enables.

android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Display Over Other Apps
Why it is needed: This permission is the foundational core of Custom Crosshair. It permits the App's floating WindowManager view to render the crosshair graphic (HUD reticle) over full-screen gaming applications.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: This permission is solely used to project a transparent visual graphic. Custom Crosshair does NOT record your screen, does NOT capture keystrokes or touch inputs, does NOT access camera/microphone, and cannot inspect or intercept content from other running applications.
android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Installed Apps Query
Why it is needed: Used exclusively for the user-facing "Favorite Games" / Quick Launch feature. This allows users to view their installed games inside the App, select favorites, and trigger direct launch shortcuts alongside their personalized crosshair setup.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: Installed application inspection occurs 100% locally and offline on your device's memory. The inventory of installed games/packages is never transmitted to any external server, developer database, or third-party tracking entity.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE Persistent Crosshair Service
Why it is needed: Ensures the crosshair overlay service remains active and is not prematurely killed by Android's background memory management or battery optimization while the user is actively playing high-demand 3D shooter games.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Quick Controls Notification
Why it is needed: Required on Android 13+ (API level 33+) to display an ongoing system notification associated with the foreground crosshair service. This notification provides players with immediate, one-tap controls to toggle visibility, adjust position, or instantly terminate the overlay service without switching out of their game.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: Notifications are strictly operational controls. We do not send marketing, spam, or promotional push notifications.

5. Local Storage (Device Only)

Custom Crosshair uses standard Android SharedPreferences to save your configuration preferences directly on your device storage. This includes:

  • Crosshair geometry, scale, line thickness, rotation, and center dot settings.
  • Color codes (HEX/ARGB) and opacity/transparency values.
  • Selected crosshair style / reticle preset.
  • Selected favorite games for the quick launcher list.

All such configuration parameters remain stored offline in the App's sandboxed local data partition. If you uninstall the application or clear app data via Android Settings, these local preferences are permanently removed.

8. Your Privacy Rights & Opt-Out

Depending on your country or state of residence, you may have specific privacy rights under laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA / CPRA).

These rights include:

  • Right to Access & Know: You may request information on what categories of technical data are processed.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request the deletion of personal information held by us (subject to legal exceptions).
  • Right to Opt Out of Targeted Advertising: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising Identifier (AAID) and opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time through standard Android system settings:
  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and select Google (or Privacy & Security > Ads on newer versions).
  3. Tap on Ads.
  4. Select Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID, or toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Once opted out, you may still receive advertisements served by AdMob, but they will be contextual (based on general app category or approximate region) rather than tailored to your interests or cross-app browsing behavior.
