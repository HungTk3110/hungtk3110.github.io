2. Information We Collect

We design Smart Clean: Phone Storage to provide storage optimization and device maintenance without collecting unnecessary personal information. The Application does not require account creation, login credentials, or personal contact details.

A. Information Provided Directly by You

You are not required to provide personal information to use the Application. However, if you choose to contact us for customer support or feedback via email, we will receive:

  • Your email address.
  • The contents of your message and any diagnostic screenshots you choose to share.

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiries and troubleshoot technical issues.

B. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To calculate space savings, detect duplicate files, and remove system clutter, the Application processes storage metadata strictly on your local device:

  • File Metadata: Temporary cache paths, residual folders from previously removed apps, obsolete APK packages, empty folders, and file checksums for duplicate file identification. None of your files, photos, or documents are ever uploaded to external servers.
  • System & Memory Metrics: Memory (RAM) utilization, internal storage capacity, and battery temperature to display real-time dashboard meters and facilitate Phone Boost operations.

C. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

When you access the Application on an Android device, certain technical and diagnostic data may be collected automatically by integrated third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) and operating system services:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), unique device identifiers, IP address, and network carrier information.
  • Hardware Specifications: Device model, manufacturer, screen resolution, operating system version, and system language.
  • Diagnostic Telemetry: Crash stack traces, ANR (Application Not Responding) logs, and performance metrics.
  • Coarse Location Data: Approximate, country- or city-level location derived from IP addresses (precise GPS location is not requested or collected).

6. Android Permissions & Justifications

In compliance with the Google Play Permissions Policy, the following section discloses every sensitive and runtime Android permission requested by the Application:

android.permission.MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE All Files Access
Why it is needed: Smart Clean's core purpose is comprehensive device storage optimization. Standard scoped storage limits an application to its own private directory, which prevents it from locating and cleaning residual junk files left by uninstalled applications, obsolete APK install packages, empty directories, and app cache in shared storage.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: This permission is used exclusively for scanning and removing user-approved junk files. Smart Clean never reads, inspects, or uploads personal documents, private photos, messages, or sensitive files. File deletion occurs only upon explicit user confirmation.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notification Service
Why it is needed: Enables an optional quick-clean toolbar in the Android notification drawer for 1-tap memory boost, and delivers low-storage alerts when device storage falls below safe operational limits.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: Notifications are strictly operational controls and storage alerts. We do not send marketing spam or promotional push notifications.
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS / KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES Memory Optimization
Why it is needed: Used by the Phone Boost feature to measure active background memory usage and safely hibernate idle background tasks when the user initiates a RAM cleanup.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: Memory metrics are processed 100% locally in volatile RAM. No history of app usage or personal habits is recorded or uploaded.
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Communication
Why it is needed: Required strictly for third-party SDK communications: loading AdMob advertisements, transmitting anonymized crash traces via Firebase, and verifying subscription entitlement tokens with Google Play Billing.

9. Local Storage & Data Retention

Local Storage (SharedPreferences)

Smart Clean uses Android's private SharedPreferences to save configuration preferences locally on your hardware. This includes:

  • Selected app interface language.
  • Timestamp of the most recent junk clean and boost operations.
  • VIP / Pro entitlement activation flag (retrieved securely from Google Play Billing).
  • Notification bar toggle settings and storage alert threshold preferences.

All settings remain strictly in the Application's protected internal storage. If you clear app data in Android Settings or uninstall Smart Clean, all locally stored preferences are permanently deleted.

Data Retention & Security

Because all file inspection and cleaning algorithms run locally on your device, the developer does not retain any scanned directories, file names, or user files on external infrastructure. Third-party SDK telemetry is retained by Google LLC according to their standard retention schedules.

All network communication conducted by integrated SDKs uses standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or state of residence, you may have specific privacy rights under laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA / CPRA).

These rights include:

  • Right to Transparency: The right to know what categories of technical data are processed.
  • Right to Opt Out of Targeted Advertising: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising Identifier (GAID) and opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time through standard Android system settings:
  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and select Google (or Privacy & Security > Ads on newer versions).
  3. Tap on Ads.
  4. Select Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

Once opted out, you will still receive advertisements served by AdMob, but they will be contextual (based on general app category or coarse region) rather than tailored to your cross-app browsing behavior.
