1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Smart Clean: Phone Storage (short name: Smart Clean, Package: com.obito.smartclean) is an Android device maintenance, storage optimization, and performance utility. The application is built to help users reclaim storage space by detecting residual junk files, system and app cache, duplicate files, empty directories, and optimizing background RAM usage.

We respect your digital privacy and are dedicated to total transparency. We operate in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, specifically including the User Data Policy, Device and Network Abuse Policy, and Permissions and APIs that Access Sensitive Information Policy (notably covering MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

2. Information Collection & Processing

Explicit Privacy Declaration: The developer does NOT directly collect, sell, lease, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from users of Smart Clean.

Smart Clean operates anonymously and does not require account registration, login credentials, or social media connections. We never collect, upload, or sell:

  • Personal identity credentials (names, physical addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, usernames).
  • Real-time geographic location (Precise GPS or fine location coordinates).
  • Biometric data, contacts, messages, call logs, calendar events, or audio recordings.
  • Personal photos, confidential documents, or private media contents stored on your device.

Technical & Operational Data Handled Locally

To deliver core storage cleaning and system performance statistics, the App inspects the following non-personal data strictly on-device:

  • Storage Metadata: File extensions, file sizes, temporary cache paths, residual folders from previously removed apps, obsolete APK packages, and checksum hashes for duplicate file identification. None of this file metadata is ever uploaded to external servers.
  • Hardware & System Metrics: Total and available RAM capacity, RAM utilization percentages, battery temperature, and total/free internal storage space to render real-time dashboard meters.
  • Diagnostic Stack Traces: Non-personally identifiable crash logs and ANR (Application Not Responding) telemetry processed via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs across diverse Android phone models.

4. Android Permissions & Justifications

In accordance with Google Play's Sensitive Permissions Policy, every permission requested by Smart Clean is strictly tied to an essential user-facing feature:

android.permission.MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE All Files Access (Core Feature)
Why it is needed: Smart Clean's core purpose is comprehensive storage optimization. Standard scoped storage limits an application to its own private directory, preventing it from detecting and cleaning residual junk files left behind by uninstalled applications, obsolete APK install files, hidden temporary folders, and app cache located across shared storage volumes.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: This permission is used exclusively for scanning, listing, and removing user-approved junk files. Smart Clean never reads, inspects, modifies, or transmits personal documents, private photos, messages, or sensitive files to external servers. File deletion occurs only with the user's explicit action and consent.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Quick Notification Toolbar
Why it is needed: Enables an optional persistent quick-clean toolbar in the Android notification drawer. This allows users to monitor real-time RAM usage and trigger a 1-tap memory boost directly without launching the full application. It also provides low-storage warnings when available storage drops critically below safe thresholds.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: Notifications are strictly operational controls and storage alerts. We do NOT deliver unsolicited marketing spam or third-party promotional push messages.
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS / KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES Memory & Task Optimization
Why it is needed: Used by the "Phone Boost" module to determine active background process memory footprints and safely terminate non-essential idle background tasks when the user requests a RAM cleanup.
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: System usage statistics are processed 100% locally in device volatile memory. No history of app usage or personal habits is recorded, logged, or uploaded anywhere.
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Communication
Why it is needed: Required strictly for third-party SDK operational calls: loading AdMob advertisements, sending anonymized crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics, and verifying subscription entitlement tokens with Google Play In-App Billing servers.

5. Local Storage (Device Only)

Smart Clean relies entirely on Android's private SharedPreferences sandbox to maintain your operational preferences locally on your hardware. This includes:

  • Selected app interface language.
  • Timestamp of the most recent junk clean and boost operations.
  • VIP / Pro entitlement activation flag (retrieved securely from Google Play Billing).
  • Notification bar toggle settings and storage alert threshold preferences.

All settings remain strictly in the App's protected internal storage. If you clear the application data in Android Settings or uninstall Smart Clean, all locally stored preferences are permanently wiped from the device.

6. Data Retention & Security

Data Retention Policy

Because all file inspection and cleaning algorithms run locally on your Android device, the developer does not retain any scanned directories, file names, or user files on external infrastructure. Third-party SDK telemetry (such as crash stack traces and aggregated AdMob impressions) is retained by Google LLC according to their standard retention schedules (typically from 30 days up to 14 months for statistical aggregates).

Data Security Measures

All communication between integrated third-party SDKs and Google servers is enforced using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption. While we implement rigorous technical practices, please note that no method of electronic data transmission or wireless networking is 100% immune to potential risks.

9. Your Privacy Rights & Opt-Out

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR in the European Economic Area / UK, or the CCPA / CPRA in California), you are entitled to specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Transparency: The right to know what data categories are processed and for what operational purposes.
  • Right to Restrict or Opt Out of Ad Personalization: You may at any time reset or permanently delete your Google Advertising ID through your device's native settings.

How to Reset or Delete Advertising ID on Android:

  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Privacy & Security > Ads on newer Android versions).
  3. Tap on Ads.
  4. Select Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

When you delete or reset your Advertising ID, AdMob will serve only non-personalized, contextual advertisements based on general app characteristics and coarse country location.
